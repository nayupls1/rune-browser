Weeknight Tomato Soup

By Marta · Updated March 3 · 2,481 comments
Jump to recipe

There's something about the first cold evening of autumn that sends me straight back to my grandmother's kitchen in the valley, where the windows fogged up every October and the radio was always a little too loud.

She never measured anything, of course. When I finally asked her to write this down, she handed me a napkin with four words on it and a drawing of a pot. It took me eleven years and a lot of soup to get it right.

Before we get started, let's talk about tomatoes. You might think any tomato will do, and honestly, you're mostly right, but hear me out, because the story of how I found the right can is a long one.

Weeknight Tomato Soup

Serves 4 · 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 cans (800 g) whole tomatoes
  • 500 ml vegetable stock
  • A handful of basil

Steps

  1. Soften the onion in the oil, 8 minutes. Add the garlic for one more.
  2. Add tomatoes and stock; simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Blend with the basil, season, serve.
