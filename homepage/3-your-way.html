Grandma’s weeknight tomato soup

By Marguerite, updated 3 days ago, 14 min read

AdvertisementThis one pan does the work of nine. Chefs are furious.

The first time I made this soup it was raining, the kind of rain that makes the whole street smell like wet stone. My grandmother stood at the stove in a cardigan two sizes too big, humming something she never told me the name of.

Years later I moved across the country with one suitcase and a notebook of her recipes, most of them written on the backs of envelopes. This one was on a gas bill from 1987, which I still have, framed, above my own stove.

Before we get to the recipe, a word about tomatoes. I have strong opinions about tomatoes. Some would say too strong. My partner certainly would, and so would the man at the farmers market who now hides when he sees me coming.

Anyway, autumn is here and the soup pot is out. Here is what you will need, and a few things I learned the hard way.
