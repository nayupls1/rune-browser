A real app, not a prototype

The agent writes a real project with its own server and SQLite database, runs it in a sandbox and shows it next to the chat. Keep changing it by talking, or close the chat and just use it.

Change the websites you use

Ask about a page or point at something on it. The agent hides, restyles or adds things, on that page or the whole site, and each change gets its own switch. Bundle them into a mod to share.
