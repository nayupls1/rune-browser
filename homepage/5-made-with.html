From a sentence to an app you keep

  1. 1

    Describe it

    Open a new tab and say what you want, the way you'd tell a friend who codes.

    A habit tracker with daily check-ins and a streak count
    Claude CodeSend
  2. 2

    The agent builds it and runs it

    A real project with its own server and SQLite database, running in a sandbox, in a live preview next to the chat. It fixes what it sees there.

    • Wrote server.ts and the page
    • Added a habits table
    • Fixed a layout issue in the preview
    • Running
  3. 3

    Use it, and change it by talking

    Close the chat and it's just your app. When you want something different, ask for it.

    Make the streak count bigger and add a weekly view
    Done. The streak is at the top, and there's a Week tab.

And the rest of the browser

It changes the websites you use, too
Point at something on a page and say what you want: hidden, restyled, or something added. Each change has its own switch, per page or per site, and you can bundle them into a mod to share.
Your own agents, your own logins
It runs the coding agents you already use, signed in with your own subscription. No API keys, and no account here. Claude CodeCodexOpenCode
Safe, and nothing is lost
Apps run in a sandbox and ask before reaching anything beyond it, and your secrets never reach the agent. Every change is a snapshot you can undo, data included.
